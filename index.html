Color Palette

Six colors. Infinite warmth.

Claude's palette breaks from the typical cold blues and neon purples of tech. Instead, a warm terra cotta anchors the design, softened by pampas and grays that feel like linen and sunlight.

The result is an interface that feels calm, grounded, and distinctly human.

Design Principles

What makes it work

The Claude aesthetic prioritizes restraint, warmth, and clarity. Every choice serves the experience.

◈

Terra Cotta Accent

A single warm hue drives the entire visual hierarchy. It guides the eye without shouting.

◊

Serif-First Typography

Lora for body text signals care and scholarship. Poppins handles UI elements with clean precision.

◆

Generous Whitespace

Every element breathes. The negative space is as intentional as the content it frames.

Typography

Two fonts, one voice

Display · Lora 400
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow.
UI · Poppins 300
Clean, functional, and always legible. Built for buttons, labels, and navigation.
Body · Lora 400
Long-form text benefits from the warmth of a serif. Line heights are generous, letter spacing natural. The goal is effortless reading that respects both the content and the reader's time. This is how Claude speaks.
